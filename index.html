    Bliki: Host Leadership

That's never sounded quite right to me, and a recent conversation with Kent Beck nailed why - it's gaslighting. The manager claims to be a servant, but everyone knows who really has the power. My colleague Giles Edwards-Alexander told me about an alternative way of thinking about leadership, one that he came across working with mental-health professionals. This casts the leader as a host: preparing a suitable space, inviting the team in, providing ideas and problems, and then stepping back to let them work.
